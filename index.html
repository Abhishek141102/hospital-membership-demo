FAQ

Questions, made simple.

Common questions a prospective member may have before choosing a plan.

Still have a question? →
What is a hospital membership?

A hospital membership is an annual subscription that provides defined healthcare benefits, discounts and eligible services according to the selected plan.

How long is the membership valid?

The demo plans are shown as annual memberships. The hospital can define the actual validity and renewal rules.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Yes. A future system can support plan upgrades or downgrades according to hospital-defined terms.

Can family members be included?

Family benefits can be offered as an optional feature, especially with premium plans such as Gold.

Are all hospital services covered?

No. Coverage and discounts should always be defined clearly by the hospital for each membership plan.
